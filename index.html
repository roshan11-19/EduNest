Help & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our home tuition services.

Where are the tuition classes conducted?

Classes are conducted strictly 100% in-person at the Teacher's Residence Study located at PM Palem, Dharmapuram, Opp Living Space, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. Students attend focused evening sessions (6:30 PM to 8:30 PM / 9:00 PM). We do not conduct online video classes or recorded lessons, ensuring maximum academic focus.

Can a student register for multiple classes?

No. A student can register for only one academic class at a time (e.g., either 10th Standard or 11th Standard). However, you can choose any combination of subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) for that class.

Do I need to pay any fee online during registration?

No online payment is required on this website. Registration is completely free. Tuition fees are discussed and collected directly with the teacher after in-person consultation.

What happens after I submit the registration form?

After clicking submit, your unique Registration ID will be generated and saved. You will be directed to send a pre-filled registration slip to the teacher on WhatsApp (+91 90630 30342). The teacher will then contact you to finalize the timings and share the residence location.

Which areas/localities are nearby the teacher's residence?

Students comfortably travel from nearby residential areas across Visakhapatnam including PM Palem, Madhurawada, Dharmapuram, Kommadi, Yendada, MVP Colony, Rushikonda, and surrounding neighborhoods.
